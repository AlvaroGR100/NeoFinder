About NeoFinder

NeoFinder is a powerful tool for locating specific bedrock patterns in Minecraft worlds, making it easier to find potential base locations.

How to Use

  • Enter Seed: Input your Minecraft world seed in the seed field.
  • Adjust Grid Size: Use the width and height sliders to set your pattern size (4-32 blocks).
  • Set Y Level: Choose the height at which to search for bedrock patterns (-64 to 128).
  • Draw Pattern: Click on grid cells to mark where you expect bedrock (blue) or air (gray).
  • Find Matches: Click "Find Matches" to search for locations in your world that match your pattern.

Tips

  • Bedrock patterns at Y=-63 are commonly used for creating unique base identifiers.
  • More complex patterns yield fewer but more specific results.
  • The tool searches a large area and returns coordinates where your pattern matches.
  • Toggle between light and dark modes using the theme button.

Technical Details

NeoFinder uses the Xoroshiro128++ random number generator to accurately predict bedrock placement in Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.18+, ensuring results match your actual world.
